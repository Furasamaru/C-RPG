# ⚔️ YGGDRASIL RPG — Level Progression Guide (1–30 & Level 100 Math)

Welcome to the **YGGDRASIL Character Development Manual**. This guide covers character progression strategies, stat distribution mechanics, and recommended paths for levels **1–15** and **15–30**, built fully coherent with the max **Level 100 system ceiling**.

---

## ⚙️ Core Stat & Level Allocation Rules

To maintain coherence from Level 1 to Level 100, character progression follows three fundamental rules:

1. **The Level Cap & Class Tier Caps:** Total character level cannot exceed **100**.
    * **Basic Classes:** Max Level 15 per class.
    * **High Classes:** Max Level 10 per class *(Requires prerequisite Basic classes)*.
    * **Rare / Epithet Classes:** Max Level 5 per class *(Requires prerequisite High classes)*.
2. **Racial Level Limits:**
    * **Humanoid:** 0 Racial Levels allowed (100% Job levels).
    * **Demi-Human:** Max 30–45 Racial Levels total.
    * **Heteromorph:** Max 45 Racial Levels total.
3. **Stat Point Allocation Mechanics:**
    * Stat growth is **not distributed manually point-by-point**. Instead, each Level gained grants a **pool of stat growth points** automatically assigned to core stats based on the class chosen (e.g., *Fighter* invests growth into HP/Physical Atk, while *Mage* invests into MP/Magical Atk).
    * **Humanoids:** Receive **1 Base Stat Point** added to their pool per level. *(Max ~100 Base Stats at Level 100)*.
    * **Demi-Humans:** Receive **+2 Base Stat Points** per Racial Level, **+1** per Job Level. *(Max ~130–145 Base Stats at Level 100)*.
    * **Heteromorphs:** Receive **+3 Base Stat Points** per Racial Level, **+1** per Job Level. *(Max ~190 Base Stats at Level 100 — "Off the Chart")*.

---

<details>
<summary><b>👤 1. Humanoid Category (Humans, Elves, Dwarves)</b></summary>

> **Core Philosophy:** *Zero Racial Levels. 100% Job Class freedom.*

Humanoids receive **1 Base Stat Point** per level. They are the most versatile category, immune to systemic town restrictions and monster-hunting penalties, but have lower base stat totals than non-humanoids.

<details>
<summary><b>Levels 1–15: The Blank Canvas Phase</b></summary>

* **Build Limit:** Up to 15 levels in a single **Basic Job Class**.
* **Stat Growth:** Balanced (+1 Stat Point per level assigned to job stats).
* **Key Advantage:** Free entry to all starting cities, safe zones, and trade hubs. Zero PK target priority.

#### Recommended Starting Paths:
* **Martial Vanguard:** `Fighter [Lvl 15]`
    * *Unlocks:* Heavy armor proficiency, active combat skills (*Power Strike*, *Cleave*).
* **Arcane Scholar:** `Mage [Lvl 15]`
    * *Unlocks:* Tier 1–2 Arcane Spells (*Magic Arrow*, *Fireball*, *Shield*).
* **Shadow Stalker:** `Rogue [Lvl 15]`
    * *Unlocks:* Lockpicking, stealth maneuvers, backstab damage multipliers.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Levels 15–30: The Specialized Initiate Phase</b></summary>

* **Build Focus:** Diversifying into a 2nd Basic Job Class OR unlocking your first High Job Class (Requires prerequisite investments).
* **Stat Growth:** Continues at +1 Stat Point per level.

#### Example Level 30 Builds:
* **Paladin Initiate:** `Fighter [Lvl 15]` + `Cleric [Lvl 15]`
    * *Role:* Frontline tank with self-healing and Holy element attacks. Shifts Karma toward **+500 (Good)**.
* **Spellblade / Battle Mage:** `Fighter [Lvl 15]` + `Mage [Lvl 15]`
    * *Role:* Hybrid melee/caster. Sacrifices pure mana pool for close-range survival.
* **Assassin Trainee:** `Rogue [Lvl 15]` + `Assassin [Lvl 15]`
    * *Role:* High Agility burst attacker specializing in critical hits and status ailments.

</details>

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><b>🐾 2. Demi-Human Category (Ogres, Goblins, Lizardmen)</b></summary>

> **Core Philosophy:** *Balanced growth. Stronger physical baseline than Humanoids.*

Demi-Humans receive **+2 Stat Points per Racial Level** and **+1 Stat Point per Job Level**. They strike a balance between raw physical power and skill versatility.

<details>
<summary><b>Levels 1–15: The Racial Foundation Phase</b></summary>

* **Build Limit:** Max 15 levels in **Basic Racial Classes**.
* **Stat Growth:** Accelerated physical growth (+2 Stat Points per Racial level automatically added to physical stats).
* **Key Advantage:** Natural physical superiority (HP, Strength, Natural Armor) over early Humanoids.
* **Drawback:** Minor gear restrictions (e.g., non-standard headwear or boot slots).

#### Recommended Starting Paths:
* **Goblin Skirmisher:** `Goblin [Lvl 10]` + `Rogue [Lvl 5]`
    * *Traits:* High Agility, small hitboxes, bonus damage when fighting in packs.
* **Lizardman Warrior:** `Lizardman [Lvl 10]` + `Fighter [Lvl 5]`
    * *Traits:* Natural scale defense (+Phy Def), underwater breathing, tail strike abilities.
* **Ogre Brute:** `Ogre [Lvl 15]`
    * *Traits:* Massive HP pool and Physical Attack stats, balanced by an Agility penalty.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Levels 15–30: The Hybrid Expansion Phase</b></summary>

* **Build Focus:** Maxing out base Racial levels (15 max) and layering Job Classes for utility and active skills.

#### Example Level 30 Builds:
* **Ogre Vanguard:** `Ogre [Lvl 15]` + `Fighter [Lvl 15]`
    * *Role:* High-HP juggernaut. Out-stats level 30 Humanoid fighters in raw health and physical force.
* **Lizardman Shaman:** `Lizardman [Lvl 15]` + `Shaman [Lvl 15]`
    * *Role:* Nature caster combining elemental magic with solid melee durability.
* **Goblin Chieftain:** `Goblin [Lvl 15]` + `Commander [Lvl 15]`
    * *Role:* Party buffer using leadership skills to empower nearby allies or summoned minions.

</details>

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><b>💀 3. Heteromorph Category (Undead, Demons, Insects, Angels)</b></summary>

> **Core Philosophy:** *High Risk, High Reward. Monstrous stats and immunities.*

Heteromorphs receive **+3 Stat Points per Racial Level** and **+1 Stat Point per Job Level**. They are statutory powerhouses, but face severe racial weaknesses, city bans, and target-on-sight PK penalties.

<details>
<summary><b>Levels 1–15: The Monstrous Awakening Phase</b></summary>

* **Build Limit:** Forced into **Basic Racial Classes** to survive innate drawbacks.
* **Stat Growth:** Maximum base growth (+3 Stat Points per Racial level into HP/MP/Resistances).
* **Key Advantage:** Massive base stats, standard darkvision, and racial status immunities (e.g., Undead are immune to poison, sleep, and mind control).
* **Drawback:** Double damage from opposite elements (Fire/Holy for Undead; Holy/Ice for Demons). Banned from standard safe-zone towns.

#### Recommended Starting Paths:
* **Skeleton Caster:** `Skeleton Caster [Lvl 15]`
    * *Traits:* Darkvision, poison/bleed immunity. Vulnerable to Bludgeoning and Holy damage.
* **Imp (Infernal):** `Imp [Lvl 15]`
    * *Traits:* Flight, fire resistance, minor dark magic access; weak to Holy/Light elements.
* **Insectoid Worker:** `Insectoid [Lvl 15]`
    * *Traits:* High natural carapace defense, multi-limb attacks; reduced base MP pool.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Levels 15–30: Evolution to High Race</b></summary>

* **Build Focus:** Unlocking **High Racial Classes** (Max Level 10) or taking initial Job Classes to round out spellcasting and combat kits.

#### Example Level 30 Builds:
* **Elder Lich Initiate:** `Skeleton Caster [Lvl 15]` + `Elder Lich [Lvl 10] (High Race)` + `Mage [Lvl 5]`
    * *Role:* Evolved Undead Caster. Possesses an inflated MP pool compared to level 30 Humanoid casters, accessing Tier 3–4 Arcane and Necromancy spells early.
* **Demon Berserker:** `Imp [Lvl 15]` + `Devil [Lvl 10] (High Race)` + `Fighter [Lvl 5]`
    * *Role:* Highly mobile aerial brawler utilizing natural claws, dark buffs, and physical strikes.
* **Arachne Stalker:** `Insectoid [Lvl 15]` + `Rogue [Lvl 15]`
    * *Role:* Web-trapping assassin combining high natural carapace armor with stealth and poison abilities.

</details>

</details>
