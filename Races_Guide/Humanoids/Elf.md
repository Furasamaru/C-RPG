# 🧝 YGGDRASIL RPG — Elf Race Mechanics & Progression Manual

This document details the progression mechanics, stat scaling, and core features of the **Elf** race in the YGGDRASIL RPG system. Classified under the **Humanoid** category, Elves possess 0 Racial Levels and focus 100% of their character levels into Job Classes.

<details>
<summary><b>🧬 Race Traits & Attributes</b></summary>

Elves are graceful, long-lived humanoids naturally attuned to nature, archery, and elemental magic.

* **Humanoid Subtype:** Elves have **0 Racial Levels**. All 100 total character levels are allocated directly into Job Classes.

* **Innate Physical Passives:**

    * **Keen Senses & Darkvision:** Enhanced perception stats, allowing detection of hidden traps, stealthy targets, and night vision in low-light environments.

    * **Forest Movement:** Movement speed penalties are ignored when traversing dense forest, jungle, or uneven natural terrain.

    * **Mana Sensitivity:** Inherent boost to detecting ambient magical sources or incoming spellcasting.

* **System Access:** Elves enjoy universal entry to standard cities, trade hubs, and safe zones without town guard hostility or systemic player-killer bounties.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📊 Stat Growth Mechanics</b></summary>

As a Humanoid race, Elven base stat growth is tied entirely to active Job Class selection rather than racial level scaling.

* **Stat Pool Multiplier:** +1 Base Stat Point per Level.

* **Point Budget Formula:**

  $$
  \text{Total Base Stats} = (1 \times \text{Job Level})
  $$

* **Stat Growth Orientation:** While Elves receive the standard +1 base point per level, their natural passives synergize exceptionally well with high Agility, ranged physical, and nature/arcane casting Job Classes.

</details>

<summary><b>⚡ Key Synergies & Tactical Playstyle</b></summary>

* **Arcane Bow Synergies:** Unlocking rare classes like `Arcane Archer` allows Elves to infuse magical elemental damage (fire, ice, lightning) directly into ranged physical projectile attacks.

* **High Agility & Kiting:** Allocating all 100 levels into ranged and mobility Job classes allows Elven builds to maintain maximum distance, out-pacing enemies while providing long-range critical fire.
