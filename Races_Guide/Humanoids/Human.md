# 👤 YGGDRASIL RPG — Human Race Mechanics & Progression Manual

This document details the progression mechanics, stat scaling, and core advantages of playing a **Human** in the YGGDRASIL RPG system. Unlike Heteromorphs or Demi-Humans, Humans do not possess a racial evolution tree. Instead, their power comes from **absolute Job Class versatility**.

<details>
<summary><strong>1. Core Philosophy & The "Zero Racial Level" Rule</strong></summary>

### The Blank Slate
In YGGDRASIL, the Human race is defined by what it *lacks*: it has no natural claws, no innate magic, no darkvision, and no racial stat boosts. However, this is precisely their greatest strength.

Because Humans have **0 Racial Levels**, a Human player can invest all **100 of their character levels purely into Job Classes**.

* **No Wasted Levels:** A Heteromorph must spend 30-45 levels on Racial classes just to reach their final form. A Human can use those same 45 levels to master 3 or 4 entirely different High and Rare Job classes.
* **No Innate Weaknesses:** Humans do not suffer from the 2.0x elemental damage penalties that plague Heteromorphs (e.g., Undead taking double Holy damage).
* **Universal Access:** Humans can enter any safe-zone city without being attacked by guards and do not suffer from systemic PK (Player Killer) bounties.

<summary><strong>2. Stat Pool Distribution Logic (The Math)</strong></summary>

### Stat Growth Mechanics
Human stat growth is the slowest in the game in terms of base numbers, balanced by their access to overpowered skills and gear.

* **Stat Pool Multiplier:** +1 Base Stat Point per Level.
* **Point Budget Formula:**

  $$
  \text{Total Base Stats} = (1 \times \text{Job Level})
  $$

* **Archetype Allocation:** Unlike racial classes which force your stats in a specific direction (e.g., Skeleton Mages getting only MP/Mag Atk), a Human's stat allocation is **100% determined by their active Job Class**. Leveling `Fighter` grants points to HP and Physical Attack; leveling `Mage` grants points to MP and Magical Attack.

### Level Comparison (Human vs. Skeleton Mage)

| Level Bracket | Human Base Stats | Skeleton Mage Base Stats | Human Advantage |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **1.0 Points** | 3.0 Points | Can equip basic armor/weapons to offset stat gap. |
| **Level 15** | **15.0 Points** | 45.0 Points | Unlocks first High Job Class early. |
| **Level 100** | **100.0 Points** | 190.0 Points | Possesses 30-45 more Job Levels for elite skills. |

<summary><strong>4. Endgame & Job Evolution (Level 100)</strong></summary>

### The Equipment Reliance
Because a Human's base stats cap at **100 Points**, they will mathematically lose a naked fistfight against a Level 100 Heteromorph (who has 190+ stats).

To bridge this gap, Humans rely on:
1. **Divine-Class Gear:** Humans have zero racial restrictions on armor and weapons. A fully geared Human will use Divine items to artificially boost their stats past the 100-point limit.
2. **Skill Bloat:** A Level 100 Human has 100 Job levels. They will possess roughly 30% more active skills, buffs, and martial arts than a Heteromorph, allowing them to out-maneuver and out-buff their opponent.

### Key Synergy Notes
* **Limitless Potential:** Humans can pivot their builds seamlessly. If they find a rare magical artifact, they can easily invest their next 10 levels into `Mage` or `Cleric` without worrying about their physical "Racial" body restricting their magic output.
* **The ultimate "Meta" race:** In competitive YGGDRASIL guilds, Humans were highly favored for their ability to perfectly adapt to any raid mechanic through Job Class combinations and unrestricted gear swapping.
