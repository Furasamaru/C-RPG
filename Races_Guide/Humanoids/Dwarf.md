# ⛏️ YGGDRASIL RPG — Dwarf Race Mechanics & Progression Manual

This document details the progression mechanics, stat scaling, and core features of the **Dwarf** race in the YGGDRASIL RPG system. Classified under the **Humanoid** category, Dwarves possess 0 Racial Levels and focus 100% of their character levels into Job Classes.

<details>
<summary><strong>🧬 Race Traits & Fundamental Characteristics</strong></summary>

Dwarves are sturdy, subterranean humanoids known for their high physical durability, affinity for forge-crafting, and natural resilience against harsh environmental conditions.

* **Humanoid Subtype:** Dwarves have **0 Racial Levels**. All 100 total character levels are allocated directly into Job Classes.

* **Innate Physical Passives:**

    * **Darkvision:** Allows clear vision in unlit underground environments and dungeons without requiring artificial light sources.

    * **Sturdy Frame:** Increased resistance against knockback, stagger, and forced displacement effects.

    * **Forge Affinity:** Grants inherent bonus proficiency and success-rate multipliers when using crafting, smithing, or item-enchanting Job Classes.

* **System Access:** Dwarves enjoy universal entry to standard cities, trade hubs, and safe zones without town guard hostility or systemic player-killer bounties.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>📊 Stat Growth Mechanics</strong></summary>

As a Humanoid race, Dwarven base stat growth is tied entirely to active Job Class selection rather than racial level scaling.

* **Stat Pool Multiplier:** +1 Base Stat Point per Level.

* **Point Budget Formula:**

  $$
  \text{Total Base Stats} = (1 \times \text{Job Level})
  $$

* **Stat Growth Orientation:** While Dwarves receive the standard +1 base point per level, their racial synergy leans towards physical durability, making them naturally effective when pairing with heavy armor and crafting classes.

</details>
